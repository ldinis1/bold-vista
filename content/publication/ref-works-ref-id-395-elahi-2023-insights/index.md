abstract: The apicoplast of Plasmodium falciparum is the only source of essential
  isoprenoid precursors and Coenzyme A (CoA) in the parasite. Isoprenoid precursor
  synthesis relies on the iron–sulfur cluster (FeS) cofactors produced within the
  apicoplast, rendering FeS synthesis an essential function of this organelle. Recent
  reports provide important insights into the roles of FeS cofactors and the use of
  isoprenoid precursors and CoA both inside and outside the apicoplast. Here, we review
  the recent insights into the roles of these metabolites in blood-stage malaria parasites
  and discuss new questions that have been raised in light of these discoveries.
